Privacy Policy — JobHunt

Last updated: 29 September 2026

JobHunt is a private, single-user application operated by and used exclusively by Baraa Aljabban for personal job-search automation on his own devices. It is not offered, distributed, or accessible to the public.

Data the application accesses

  • Gmail: it reads messages in the owner's own mailbox to detect account-verification codes and replies from companies he has applied to, and it sends one daily summary email to the owner's own address.
  • Google Drive: it uploads and updates documents in the owner's own Drive related to his job applications (CV documents and cover letters).

How the data is used

All accessed data is processed locally on the owner's computer and is used solely to operate his personal job-application workflow: recording which companies and roles he applied to, archiving the CV versions he sent, and reporting status to himself.

Sharing and disclosure

No data is shared, sold, or transmitted to any third party. The application communicates only with Google APIs (Gmail and Google Drive) and the job platforms the owner chooses to apply on, using credentials that belong to the owner alone.

Storage and retention

Credentials are stored locally on the owner's machine in a protected file. Copies of documents are retained in the owner's own Google Drive until he deletes them. No data is stored on any server controlled by a third party.

Security

Access tokens are held locally and are never written to logs, sent by email, or committed to any code repository. The application runs on the owner's personal computer only.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to baraa.aljabban.it@gmail.com.
